Honest Thumbnails

See a real frame from each YouTube video instead of the clickbait thumbnail.

  1. Open Safari and go to youtube.com.
  2. Tap the page menu in the address bar, then Manage Extensions.
  3. Turn on Honest Thumbnails, then allow it on youtube.com.
